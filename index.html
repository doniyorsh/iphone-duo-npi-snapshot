Takeaway

Conclusion

  • Upgrade intent is the primary demand driver, with 72% of iPhone Duo interest coming from existing iPhone owners.
  • Trade-up momentum sits with recent Pro owners — iPhone 17 and 16 Pro / Pro Max together make up 61% of upgraders.
  • Samsung is the leading switch-in source at 54%, highlighting a clear conquest opportunity around the foldable experience.
  • Star White and Night Sky are closely matched (58% / 42%), so both colours warrant deep availability; 256GB and 512GB together cover 76% of capacity demand.
  • The foldable design and new multitasking experience are the strongest purchase drivers, raised in every store — a natural lead for demonstrations.
  • The most common challenge is the move to Touch ID without Face ID, followed by first-gen durability questions — both easily addressed in-store, while 28% of customers ask about affordability options.
